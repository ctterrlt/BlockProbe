# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

BlockProbe is built by and for people who want reliable, private evidence about
gambling-site blockers. We want participation to be a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Gambling harm is personal for many contributors and users. Discuss the code and
the evidence, never the person.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, commit messages — and whenever someone represents the project in
public.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences; assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive technical criticism; critique code and evidence,
  not people.
- Never post real user data, private contact details or anyone's gambling
  activity. Evidence in reports is sanitized.
- Respect privacy: never ask someone to reveal more about their network,
  location or habits than a technical report needs.
- Do not use the project to advertise gambling services, blockers, or any other
  commercial product.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging others to gamble, or shaming people for gambling-related harm.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. Depending on severity and
history, responses are: a private warning, a temporary ban from interaction, or
a permanent ban from the project spaces. Maintainers may remove comments,
commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and will explain the
reason when it is safe to do so.

For concerns about a maintainer, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
or contact another repository owner privately.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent consequences as determined by other maintainers.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the
maintainers through GitHub's private reporting on the repository, or by opening
a minimal issue asking for a private channel if no other route is available.
All complaints are reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible. The
maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
